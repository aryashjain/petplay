TECH · PART 1 — INSIDE THE BALL 🔧

Hardware & Firmware

ESP32-C6

RISC-V MCU with Wi-Fi 6 + BLE 5. Tiny, cheap, and has deep-sleep modes that sip power.

MPU 6-axis IMU

3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyroscope on I²C. Feels bumps (±16 g) and spins (±2000 °/s).

Small LiPo battery

Rechargeable cell, fully sealed inside a chew-proof ball. The sleep mode keeps it alive for days.
